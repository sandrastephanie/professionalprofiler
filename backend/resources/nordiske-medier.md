## the role
i was responsible for all demand and lead generation, on organic and paid channels, for a broad palette of B2B niche media and research software. in practice i was the data and SEO lead: analytics and tracking, paid and organic media, content planning, cross-channel B2B campaigns, lead generation, and CRO (research and user journeys).

## what i did
- **led a complex GA3 to GA4 migration across multiple markets**, with custom properties for each market, country and customer segment
- **built anonymised Looker Studio dashboards for 25+ industries across 3 countries**, so every market could make data-driven decisions
- **built a simple website retargeting flow for under 5k a month that brought in ~50 leads a month**
- owned the full technical SEO strategy and the tracking infrastructure

## why it matters
this is where i got properly technical with data. setting up tracking from scratch across markets taught me to ask where data comes from and what happens when it breaks - questions i still ask as a PM.
