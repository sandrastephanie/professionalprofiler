## the role
Corpay One is an accounts payable / bill pay product for SMEs. for the first year and a half i ran marketing and communications solo (with strong collaboration from the agency Sparkforce), covering everything from strategy to execution: demand and lead generation, sales processes and automation in HubSpot, agency and freelancer management, marketing and product alignment, data and analytics, website maintenance and CRO, PR, events and webinars, and partner and affiliate marketing.

the short version: i turned a scale-up marketing setup into an enterprise-worthy machine.

## the numbers
- **doubled navigation conversion rate** on the website through UX best practice - from **3.38% to 6.32%**
- **increased overall leads by 5%**
- **decreased cost per lead by 6%**
- **increased partner wallet share from 53% to 60%**
- contributed directly to the **two strongest-performing sales months in the company's history**, through targeted campaigns, sharper messaging and closer alignment with the sales team

## what i built
- a refreshed brand identity and a light rebrand, with a branding guideline
- content pillars and an evergreen content bank that kept supporting lead generation and brand awareness after i left
- a complete marketing-to-sales handover process
- events and PR activities, planned and executed

## what it taught me
it was fast-paced and it tested every skill i had - and gave me a few new ones. i learned to move quickly, adapt on the fly and get close to the accounts payable side of finance, which later made the move into product feel natural.
