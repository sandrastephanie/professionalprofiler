## the angles i've seen it from
i've worked across most of the money flow in a danish SME:
- **accounts payable** - paying bills, at Corpay One
- **EDI and supply-chain documents** - orders, invoices and despatch advice moving between businesses and ERP systems, at iEDI
- **accounts receivable and debt collection** - getting paid, reminders, collection, enforcement court and the accounting logic underneath, at Likvido (Visma)

so i understand how invoices move through a business, where finance teams end up doing things manually, where communication breaks down, and how much value there is in getting the right information to the right person at the right time.

## the details i've worked with
interest calculations, legal processes, compliance, payment providers and API partners, ERP and accounting integrations (including Microsoft Business Central), bookkeeping, invoicing, payments and reconciliation.

## the danish market
i know the main players and that it's a relationship-driven market. the same names keep coming up as partners, competitors and integrations.

## what interests me next
where AI can make financial processes more proactive, rather than just automating the steps that already exist.
