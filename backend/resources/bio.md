## Sandra Stephanie Raveendran

Versatile commercial profile with an MSc in IT Product Design and 6 years of experience navigating the intersection of UX, marketing automation, and fintech. Expert at translating technical complexity into commercial clarity and building the 'engine room' for B2B SaaS growth. Specialised in driving product-led growth through deep data analytics and a people-first mindset rooted in techno-anthropology.

I have extensive knowledge and can juggle multiple responsibilities. I believe I am what is jokingly referred to as an octopus - and professionally called an "M" shaped marketeer. I can work on both strategic and executing levels, and I often conduct qualitative and quantitative user research to identify 'edge cases' in complex industries, translating insights into pragmatic product improvements.

Based in Copenhagen.
