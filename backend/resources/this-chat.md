## what it is
instead of sending you a PDF, i'd rather let you ask what you actually want to know. this chat answers as me, based on my experience, my numbers and how i work.

## how it came about
the idea and the original stack come from my former colleague at iEDI, Michael Ringdal, who built it for himself. i loved the idea, and with his OK i forked it and made it my own with Claude Code - my content, my voice and my way of working.

## how it works (the short version)
a Next.js frontend, a FastAPI backend and a language model that looks things up in a set of files about my work before it answers. you get access through a personal invitation, so i know who i'm talking to.
