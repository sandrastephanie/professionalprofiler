## the short version
i'm a product manager in fintech and B2B SaaS, based in copenhagen. what drives me is taking something complex and making it simple, for the people using a product and for the team building it. i love tech for the autonomy it gives me: when i have an idea, i can prototype and build it myself.

## how i got here
my career has grown outward rather than in a straight line. i started in copywriting and SEO for banks and SaaS companies, ran marketing in fintech, and moved into product. along the way i've worked across most of the money flow in a business - payments, accounts payable, EDI and debt collection - which gives me a broad view of how financial products fit together.

## ambition
i'm ambitious and i like to keep learning. when i've chosen to leave a job, it's been because someone offered me something more challenging.

## building things myself
with AI tools like Claude Code i've gone from writing tickets to making PRs myself, and i've built three websites on my own. it makes me a more useful partner for engineers.

## outside work
i travel solo as often as i can and have been to more than a dozen countries. it's how i reset, and it keeps me curious about people and how they do things differently. i also mentor women in tech and am starting a webinar series teaching women to build with Claude Code.

## hobbies (only if asked)
i read a lot, go to a lot of tech and community events, and still play the sims.
